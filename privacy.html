Privacy Policy for "Noti"

This Privacy Policy describes how "Noti" (hereinafter, "Noti" or "the Application"), the sole and exclusive property of **The Application Owner**, manages its users' information. At Noti, your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Noti Privacy Principles

  • No Personal Data Collection: Noti is designed to operate without collecting any personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.) from its users. We do not ask you to register, log in, or provide personal data to use the application.
  • Technical Infrastructure: The application uses technical infrastructure to facilitate RSS feed retrieval and display. Any technical data processed is strictly limited to what is necessary to provide the service and is not used for commercial purposes, marketing, profiling, or data monetization.
  • No Advertising: Noti does not contain any form of advertising. Therefore, we do not use ad tracking services that may collect data for advertising purposes.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Noti does not collect, store, or process the following personal or usage information:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact data.
  • IP addresses or persistent device identifiers.
  • Geographic location.
  • Reading habits, visited articles, or interactions within the application.
  • Any other information that could identify you directly or indirectly.

3. Technical Data Processing

For Noti to function, the application processes certain technical data necessary to retrieve and display RSS feeds. This includes:

  • RSS feed URLs that you select from the available directory.
  • Technical data required for the application to operate properly.

Any data processed is used solely for technical purposes to provide the RSS feed aggregation service. The Application Owner does not use this data for commercial purposes, user profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond technical service delivery.

4. Third-Party Services

Although Noti does not collect your personal data, when using the application, you may interact with third-party services in the following ways:

  • RSS Feeds: The application accesses public RSS feeds from news websites directly. Data collection and processing by these news websites are governed by their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the RSS sources you select from the application's directory.
  • Opening Articles in External Browser: When you tap an article, it opens in your device's default web browser (such as Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android), or in an in-app browser (such as `SFSafariViewController` or Chrome Custom Tabs). These browsers and the websites you visit may collect information according to their own privacy policies and your device settings. The Application Owner has no control over data collection by these third parties.
  • Distribution Platforms: The platforms through which you download the application (such as Google Play Store or Apple App Store) may collect usage data, transactions, or device information in accordance with their own privacy policies. These platforms operate autonomously and independently of the will and control of The Application Owner.

5. Information Security

Since Noti does not collect or store your personal data for commercial purposes, and only processes minimal technical data necessary for service operation, the risk of data breaches involving your personal information is minimal. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect our technical infrastructure. We recommend keeping your device secure and updated to protect any data processed by the application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Application Owner may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom. We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Continued use of Noti after any modification to this Privacy Policy will constitute acceptance of such changes.
